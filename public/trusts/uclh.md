## 2. Executive Pay Answer
Under the 2026/27 NHS Agenda for Change pay deal (+3.3%), healthcare staff at **University College London Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust** receive Inner London (+20%, up to £8,746). A newly qualified Band 5 nurse starts at **£38,488/year gross** (approx. **£2,384 net/month** in take-home pay after PAYE tax, National Insurance, and 8.3% NHS CARE pension deductions).
