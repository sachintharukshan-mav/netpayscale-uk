## 1. Key Trust Facts

- **ODS Code:** RWH

- **Region / Health Board:** Fringe (East of England)
- **Allowance Zone (HCAS):** Fringe Zone (+5%, up to £2,270)
- **Starting Band 5 Gross Pay:** £33,677/year
- **Starting Band 5 Net Pay:** ~£2,166/month
- **Clinical Workforce:** 6,000+ staff
- **Hospital Sites (2):** Lister Hospital (Stevenage), New Queen Elizabeth II Hospital

## 2. Executive Pay Answer
Under the 2026/27 NHS Agenda for Change pay deal (+3.3%), healthcare staff at **East and North Hertfordshire NHS Trust** receive Fringe Zone (+5%, up to £2,270). A newly qualified Band 5 nurse starts at **£33,677/year gross** (approx. **£2,166 net/month** in take-home pay after PAYE tax, National Insurance, and 8.3% NHS CARE pension deductions).
