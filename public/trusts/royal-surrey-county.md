## 2. Executive Pay Answer
Under the 2026/27 NHS Agenda for Change pay deal (+3.3%), healthcare staff at **Royal Surrey County Hospital NHS Foundation Trust** receive Fringe Zone (+5%, up to £2,270). A newly qualified Band 5 nurse starts at **£33,677/year gross** (approx. **£2,166 net/month** in take-home pay after PAYE tax, National Insurance, and 8.3% NHS CARE pension deductions).
