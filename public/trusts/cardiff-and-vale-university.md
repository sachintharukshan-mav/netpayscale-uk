- **Allowance Zone (HCAS):** Welsh NHS (GIG Cymru)
- **Starting Band 5 Gross Pay:** £32,073/year
- **Starting Band 5 Net Pay:** ~£2,080/month
- **Clinical Workforce:** 14,500+ staff
- **Hospital Sites (2):** University Hospital of Wales (Heath Park, Cardiff), University Hospital Llandough

## 2. Executive Pay Answer
Under the 2026/27 NHS Agenda for Change pay deal (+3.3%), healthcare staff at **Cardiff and Vale University Health Board** receive Welsh NHS (GIG Cymru). A newly qualified Band 5 nurse starts at **£32,073/year gross** (approx. **£2,080 net/month** in take-home pay after PAYE tax, National Insurance, and 8.3% NHS CARE pension deductions).
