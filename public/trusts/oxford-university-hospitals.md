## 1. Key Trust Facts
- **Trust Name:** Oxford University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust (Oxford University Hospitals)
- **ODS Code:** RTH
- **Geographic Area:** Oxford & Banbury
- **Region / Health Board:** South East
- **Allowance Zone (HCAS):** Standard National (No HCAS)
- **Starting Band 5 Gross Pay:** £32,073/year
- **Starting Band 5 Net Pay:** ~£2,080/month
- **Clinical Workforce:** 14,500+ staff
- **Hospital Sites (4):** John Radcliffe Hospital, Churchill Hospital, Nuffield Orthopaedic Centre, Horton General Hospital (Banbury)

## 2. Executive Pay Answer
Under the 2026/27 NHS Agenda for Change pay deal (+3.3%), healthcare staff at **Oxford University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust** receive Standard National (No HCAS). A newly qualified Band 5 nurse starts at **£32,073/year gross** (approx. **£2,080 net/month** in take-home pay after PAYE tax, National Insurance, and 8.3% NHS CARE pension deductions).
