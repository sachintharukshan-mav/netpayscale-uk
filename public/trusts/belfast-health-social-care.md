## 1. Key Trust Facts
- **Trust Name:** Belfast Health and Social Care Trust (Belfast Trust (HSC))
- **ODS Code:** BHSCT
- **Geographic Area:** Belfast, Northern Ireland
- **Region / Health Board:** Northern Ireland
- **Allowance Zone (HCAS):** HSC Northern Ireland Standard
- **Starting Band 5 Gross Pay:** £32,073/year
- **Starting Band 5 Net Pay:** ~£2,080/month
- **Clinical Workforce:** 20,000+ staff
- **Hospital Sites (4):** Royal Victoria Hospital (Belfast), Belfast City Hospital, Mater Infirmorum Hospital, Musgrave Park Hospital

## 2. Executive Pay Answer
Under the 2026/27 NHS Agenda for Change pay deal (+3.3%), healthcare staff at **Belfast Health and Social Care Trust** receive HSC Northern Ireland Standard. A newly qualified Band 5 nurse starts at **£32,073/year gross** (approx. **£2,080 net/month** in take-home pay after PAYE tax, National Insurance, and 8.3% NHS CARE pension deductions).
