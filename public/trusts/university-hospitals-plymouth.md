## 2. Executive Pay Answer
Under the 2026/27 NHS Agenda for Change pay deal (+3.3%), healthcare staff at **University Hospitals Plymouth NHS Trust** receive Standard National (No HCAS). A newly qualified Band 5 nurse starts at **£32,073/year gross** (approx. **£2,080 net/month** in take-home pay after PAYE tax, National Insurance, and 8.3% NHS CARE pension deductions).
